Key players in the history of astronomy

  • Ptolemy
    • Proposed a very detailed geocentric model.
  • Copernicus
    • Poposed a coherent heliocentric model.
  • Tycho Brahe and Johannes Kepler
    • Brahe was an excellent observational astronomer.
    • Kepler was an excellent theorist who used Brahe's data.
  • Galileo Galilei
    • Promoted the heliocentric model.
    • Synthesized the scientific method.
